Privacy Policy

AG Premier Transportation

Last updated: September 2, 2026

AG Premier Transportation respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, protected, retained, and disclosed when you use our website, mobile application, booking services, or contact us.

Information We Collect

When you request transportation or contact us, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, telephone number, pickup and drop-off locations, trip date and time, passenger information, vehicle or service preferences, and other trip details or messages you submit.

How We Use Information

We use information to process and manage transportation requests, communicate with customers about trips, provide customer support, operate and improve our services, maintain business records, protect against misuse or fraud, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our booking, communication, hosting, or transportation services, or when disclosure is required by law.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for transportation services, customer support, business records, legal requirements, and legitimate business purposes. Customers may contact us to request deletion of their personal information, subject to information we must retain for legal or legitimate business purposes.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Third-Party Services

Our website or application may rely on third-party providers for hosting, communications, database services, or other functionality. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about your personal information or request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
